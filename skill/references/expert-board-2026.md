# Expert Upside / Value Board — 2026

Running log of players flagged by analysts David shares. **Append, don't replace.** Each entry: who, the expert's ADP, the case in one line, and how it maps onto David's board and his room.

## Why national ADP understates value in TX2025

**National ADP is 1QB. TX2025 starts two.** Sixteen QBs were gone by pick 40 in the Aug 24 mock, and every one of those picks pushes a skill player down. Observed effect: the three receivers below carry national half-PPR ADPs of 62.7 / 67.7 / 73 and went at **88 / 99 / 119** in a TX2025 mock.

**So any national value call is amplified here — the discount the analyst describes, plus another 25-45 picks of drift.** When quoting an expert's ADP to David, always convert: "national X, your room ~X+30."

The converse also holds: a player the national market *fades* may still be expensive in this room if he's a QB.

**David's own framing, Aug 24:** *"Value is only important when a player's ADP is way lower than it should be, and that can change as more drafts happen."* So ADP-based value calls decay — re-check any entry older than ~a week against current ADP before acting.
