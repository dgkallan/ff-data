# Trade Value Charts — TX2025 and Empty Stadiums

Generated by `scripts/trade_chart.py` from FantasyPros consensus projections, rescored under each league's actual rules. **Regenerate in-season with rest-of-season projections** — these are preseason and go stale the moment real games are played.

## Why not just use a public chart

KeepTradeCut, FantasyCalc and Stats Guy are all dynasty and all generic scoring. Neither league is either. The edge is not out-evaluating FantasyPros — their ECR aggregates 100+ analysts and will beat any solo read. The edge is **arithmetic they cannot do because they do not know these leagues.** Convert their projections; do not second-guess their player evaluation.

## How the number is built

**1. Value is measured against the wire, not against a rank.** A player is worth the lineup points you lose if he vanishes and you refill from free agency. This makes value league-specific automatically. Replacement comes from roster math — teams x roster size allocated by position — not from a convention.

**2. The package premium lives in the curve.** Across thousands of community-judged trades, bolting a correction onto the total at verdict time agreed with human judgment 42-60% of the time; shaping the value curve itself scored 85%. Experienced managers demand a package beat a star's paper value by 15-40%. So values are raised to an exponent of 1.30, which prices a 2-for-1 at equal raw value **23% in the star's favor**.

**3. The chart is a screen, not a verdict.** Values are path-dependent — a fourth good receiver is worth far less to a roster already starting three. Use the chart to decide whether a deal is worth modelling. The verdict always comes from one before/after optimal-lineup comparison **on both rosters**. Never sum chart values and declare a winner.

## The finding that matters most

**Josh Allen is the #3 asset in TX2025 (97.5) and the #11 asset in Empty Stadiums (47.1)** — despite scoring 472 in Empty Stadiums, the highest total of any player in either league.

The reason is entirely replacement level. TX2025 starts 24 QBs across 12 teams and the wire falls to **175**; Allen's marginal value is 261. Empty Stadiums has eleven quarterbacks projecting above 407, pushing its wire to **360**; Allen's marginal value is only 112.

**Never quote a cross-league value.** A quarterback offer that looks generous in one room is an insult in the other.

## Two known gaps in the Empty Stadiums chart

**IDP is missing.** The FantasyPros public API returns QB/RB/WR/TE only. With solo tackles at 2.2 and sacks/INTs at 8, a three-down linebacker clears 250 points and belongs on this chart. Use `scripts/trade_value.py`, which already models IDP, until an IDP projection source is wired in.

**Return yards are missing.** At 0.1/yard and 10/TD, the skill calls this the largest unpriced edge in the league. The projections carry no return stats, so any returner is undervalued here by roughly 20-40 points.

## Using it during the season

```bash
# preseason
python3 scripts/trade_chart.py --top 60

# in-season: swap in rest-of-season projections, same shape
python3 scripts/trade_chart.py --proj ros-week8.json --league tx2025
```

Refresh the projections weekly via `/opt/ff/refresh.sh`. **Also update `rostered` in the script once rosters are set** — the preseason numbers are draft-based estimates, and by Week 4 the real count of rostered players at each position is knowable and will move every value on the chart.
