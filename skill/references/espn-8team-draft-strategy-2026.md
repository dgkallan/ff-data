## The Punchline

**Round 1: Take whichever of Jahmyr Gibbs / Bijan Robinson is left at 1.02. Then attack the 2/3 turn (picks 15 + 18) with an elite TE (McBride or Bowers) + best available WR/RB. Wait on QB until rounds 5–7 — the position is deep and this league is shallow.**

In an 8-team league, championships come from an elite starting lineup, not depth. The waiver wire will be loaded all season. Draft studs and upside; skip floor plays.

## Round 1 (Pick 2): Gibbs or Bijan — You Can't Lose

Whoever goes 1.01, take the other. The 2026 consensus has these two clearly ahead of the field.

**Jahmyr Gibbs (DET)** — slight consensus #1:
- Most TDs before age 24 in NFL history (49); led the NFL with 38 total scores over the last two seasons
- David Montgomery traded to Houston → Dan Campbell has called Gibbs the bell cow (Isiah Pacheco is a non-threat)
- After Week 10 last year: 19% target share, ~48 receiving yards/game — led all RBs in those categories down the stretch
- Detroit has the #1 Vegas implied scoring offense and the league's easiest schedule

**Bijan Robinson (ATL)**:
- Most scrimmage yards before age 24 all-time (5,648); RB2 in PPG last year
- Tyler Allgeier gone → goal-line work (his one 2025 weakness, 49% red-zone rush share) should spike
- Concern: Atlanta's offense projects near the bottom of the league (tied 24th in Vegas implied points) vs. Detroit's #1

**If you get a choice (1.01 takes a WR or CMC): take Gibbs.** Offense environment breaks the tie.

## Rounds 2–3 (Picks 15 + 18): The Double-Tap

In an 8-team league, the consensus top ~14 will be gone. Realistic board at 15–18 (per Field Yates/ESPN and FantasyPros consensus):

**Likely available:** Kenneth Walker III (KC), Trey McBride (TE1), Drake London, Brock Bowers (TE2), Rashee Rice, Josh Jacobs — plus one or two fallers from the top 14 (Chase Brown, CeeDee Lamb, Justin Jefferson).

**Preferred pair (in order):**

1. **Elite TE + best WR:** McBride or Bowers + London/Rice/faller WR. In a shallow league, an elite TE is a weekly positional hammer — the drop-off after Bowers/McBride is a cliff, and your opponents will be starting streamers. This is the classic shallow-league edge play.
2. **Faller WR + RB2:** If Jefferson or Lamb falls to 15, take him, then KW3/Jacobs at 18.
3. **Double WR:** If both TEs are gone by 15 (unlikely in 8-team), take the two best WRs.

**Avoid:** QB at 15/18. Even Josh Allen (ADP ~19–29) is a pass here — see QB plan below.

## Cheat-Sheet Rules for Live Draft

1. **Never pass Gibbs/Bijan at 2.** No WR, no CMC (age/injury history), no cute picks.
2. **At every turn, plan the pair** — you pick twice in ~60 seconds at 15/18, 31/34, 47/50.
3. **Tiers over ADP.** When a tier is about to break (e.g., last elite TE), act; otherwise take best available.
4. **Bye weeks matter less in 8-team** — waivers cover byes. Ignore clustering except QB+TE.
5. **When in doubt: upside.** An 8-team title is won by ceilings, not floors.
6. **Watch the room:** if a QB run starts early (rounds 3–4), do NOT follow — the Tier 2 QB you get at 50 scores within ~2 pts/game of Allen.
